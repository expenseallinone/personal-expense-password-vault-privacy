Depending on the features you use, the application may process information that you provide directly while using the app.

  • Account and login information.
  • Username or profile information.
  • Mobile number, where provided by you.
  • Password-manager information entered by you, such as website names, usernames, passwords, categories, and notes.
  • Income and expense records entered by you.
  • Expense categories, dates, amounts, and notes.

  • Payment-related information required to process transactions through third-party payment service providers.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application may be used to provide and operate the features of Personal Expense & Password Vault, including:

  • Providing password-management functionality.
  • Managing income and expense records.
  • Displaying financial summaries and charts.
  • Generating PDF reports when requested by you.
  • Providing backup and synchronization features when enabled by you.
  • Maintaining application security and authentication.
  • Processing payments for applicable services or features.
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the application.

3. Password Manager Information

Personal Expense & Password Vault provides password-management functionality. Password information entered by you is sensitive information. We use available security mechanisms to protect stored password information.

You should keep your master password, PIN, authentication information, and device secure. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and device.

4. Local Storage and Database

The application may store application data locally on your Android device using a local database. Local storage may include password records, income records, expense records, categories, and other information required for the application's functionality.

5. Cloud Backup and Synchronization

If you enable cloud backup or synchronization features, selected application data may be processed through the services used to provide those features.

Cloud synchronization is provided only when the relevant feature is used or enabled by you. If you do not use cloud backup or synchronization, your data may remain stored locally on your device.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, such as authentication, cloud synchronization, secure storage, analytics, payment processing, or other technical services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party service that you choose to use.

7. Payment Gateway - Razorpay

COLLECTION DIARY uses Razorpay as a third-party payment gateway to process applicable payments.

When you make a payment through Razorpay, information necessary to process and verify the transaction may be collected and processed by Razorpay. This may include information such as your name, mobile number, email address, transaction details, payment method, and other information required for payment processing, fraud prevention, compliance, and transaction security.

COLLECTION DIARY does not directly store complete debit card or credit card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, OTP, or other sensitive payment authentication credentials entered on Razorpay's payment interface.

Payment transactions processed through Razorpay are subject to Razorpay's own terms and privacy practices.

Razorpay Privacy Policy: https://razorpay.com/privacy-policy/
